Project panel

The artwork slot for each project. Until you add screenshots it holds the placeholder drawings; in code, swap the img source and keep the markup.

Inset · default, for dark-theme screenshots
Bleed · for artwork that fills the panel
  • Box: 3:2 (aspect-ratio: 3 / 2), --surface backdrop, --bw --border edge, --r-lg corners, overflow hidden.
  • Inset: the screenshot sits in a frame --panel-inset (5% of the panel width) from every edge. The frame has --r-sm corners, a --border edge and a --bg fill, so a dark screenshot still has a visible edge on the dark backdrop. The frame is about 16:10.
  • Screenshot: export 16:10 at 1600 × 1000 px (2× the largest frame), dark theme, WebP or AVIF. It fills the frame with object-fit: cover anchored left top, so the app’s top-left corner (its title and controls) never gets cropped.
  • Bleed: no frame; the image fills the panel at 3:2 with the same fit. Use it only for art with its own edges.
  • Sticky: above 860px the panel sits in grid columns 8–12 with position: sticky; top: var(--s-6) and align-self: start, so it stays beside the text while the longer text scrolls. At 860px and below it is static and follows the text.
  • Hover: hovering anywhere on the project turns the edge --border-strong and scales the image to --zoom over --dur-slow; no zoom with reduced motion.
  • Alt text: placeholders use alt="". Real screenshots carry information, so describe each one (for example, the tracer’s web UI showing a trace graph).

Fonts

Two families from one superfamily (Vercel’s Geist, SIL Open Font License), within the limit of two families plus one monospace. No other weights or styles are used: no italics, no 300 or 700. Fallback stacks are --font-sans and --font-mono. For the Astro site, self-host through Fontsource instead of linking Google Fonts.
